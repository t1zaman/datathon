Key insight

Big balances hide thin cushions

The highest earners have the most dollars saved but the fewest months those savings can cover. Bigger lifestyles burn through bigger balances.

The warning shows up before the loan

  • Not chance: about 1 in 1028 odds (Fisher exact test).
  • Not small fee overdrafts: the typical low point was −$1,564.

Methodology, data quality & assumptions

Sources: supplied monthly_summary.csv (185,326 rows) and loans.csv (682 loans), derived from the PKDD’99 Financial dataset. Historical observations span 2020–2025. Profiles include all 4,500 accounts.

Definitions: I = median money_in; S = median money_out; H = median household_bills; F = median of the monthly sum household_bills + insurance + loan_payments. B = mean avg_daily_balance over the account’s last six rows, sorted by month. F is descriptive and is not deducted again in runway.

Calculation: remaining buffer = B − one-off cost − expense multiple × S. Gap = S + bill increase × H − retained inflow × I. Nonpositive remaining buffer → zero runway; positive buffer with gap ≤ 0 → no shortfall (120+); otherwise min(buffer/gap,120). Threshold comparisons use unrounded values. A finite capped result and no shortfall are distinct in the export.

Groups: inflow quarters are fixed from the full dataset and do not change when filtering. Running loan means C/D; the other group includes A/B and accounts without loan records. Historical associations are not causal or predictive claims. Multiple account users are not counted as separate accounts.

Coverage: 76 accounts end before December 2025. Each profile uses its own available history, between 12 and 72 monthly rows. The buffer is historical, not a live balance. Missing future months are not invented.

Limits: cash withdrawals are not confirmed discretionary purchases. Zero recorded bills need not mean zero actual expenses. Spending shares use aggregate outflows and are full-dataset figures, explicitly unaffected by the cohort filters.

Preparation: validated required values, finite numbers, account/month uniqueness and loan links; retained zero values and negative balances. Profiles are computed by the Squirrel Away engine at load time (/api/insights/data), so no account data is stored in this page or the repo. No external chart library, and no account identifiers sent to another service. Exported files contain account-level data.
